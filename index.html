WHY CHOOSE BASELOK

BaseLok® Engineering and Technical Support for Design and Submittal Process

Collaborative Value Engineering and Site-Specific Design for Contractors and Geotechnical Engineers.
Choosing subgrade stabilization requires balancing performance, cost, and structural reliability. The BaseLok product line by Industrial Fabrics, Inc. replaces unpredictable ground improvement with mathematically verifiable mechanical stabilization.
To bridge the gap between design and jobsite execution, our dedicated BaseLok Engineering Team provides technical consultation and custom design recommendations from project conception through construction. 
Our mechanical stabilization guidance provides clear engineering and financial advantages:
  • Eliminate Chemical Stabilization: Remove the high cost, environmental liabilities, and mixing complexities associated with traditional lime or cement subgrade treatment.
  • Reduce Aggregate Thickness: Optimize load distribution to decrease necessary aggregate fill volumes, lowering excavation, haul-in, and material costs.
  • Expedite Construction Timelines: Utilize rapid, straightforward installation processes to accelerate your overall project schedule.
  • Extend Asset Service Life: Prevent long-term differential settlement, minimize rut depth, and increase the bearing capacity of roads, railways, and foundations.
