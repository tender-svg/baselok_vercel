Home Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Cookies We Use

We use one first-party cookie, set only when a staff member logs into our content-management dashboard. Regular website visitors never receive this cookie.

CookiePurposeDuration
admin_tokenKeeps an authorized staff member signed in to the content-management dashboard.24 hours, or until logout

2. Browser Storage (Local/Session Storage)

In addition to cookies, we use your browser's local and session storage — data that stays on your device and is not sent to any third party — for the following purposes only:

  • Performance caching: a temporary copy of page content so pages you've already visited load instantly, and a fallback copy so pages still display correctly if our server is briefly unreachable.
  • Display preferences: remembering whether you last viewed a page in grid or list layout.
  • Session tracking (staff only): letting our own staff know they're already logged in across browser tabs.

None of this browser storage contains your name, IP address, or any information that identifies you personally, and none of it is shared with third parties.

3. Third-Party Trackers & Advertising

We do not currently use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, advertising cookies, or any other third-party tracking technology on this website. If that changes in the future, this policy and our cookie banner will be updated accordingly.

4. Information You Submit to Us

When you use our Contact form, the information you enter (such as your name, email, phone number, company, and message) is sent directly to our team by email so we can respond to your inquiry. It is not stored in a public-facing database, sold, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. California Privacy Rights (CCPA/CPRA)

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CCPA/CPRA), gives you certain rights over personal information collected about you.

We do not sell or share your personal information (as those terms are defined under the CCPA/CPRA) with any third party, and we do not use it for cross-context behavioral advertising. Because we do not sell or share personal information, there is currently no third-party opt-out to exercise — but you can review or change your cookie preferences at any time using the "Cookie Preferences" link in our website footer.

Subject to certain exceptions, California residents have the right to:

  • Know what personal information we have collected about you and how it has been used;
  • Delete personal information we have collected from you, subject to certain legal exceptions;
  • Correct inaccurate personal information we maintain about you;
  • Opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information (not applicable today, as described above); and
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising any of these rights.

To submit a request to know, delete, or correct your information, please email info@baselok.com with the subject line "California Privacy Request," or use our Contact page. We will need to verify your identity before completing your request, and will respond within the timeframe required by law.

6. Your Choices

You can accept or decline non-essential cookies using the banner shown on your first visit to this site, and change that choice at any time via the "Cookie Preferences" link in the footer. Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies and clear local storage through their own privacy settings.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy or how we handle information submitted through our website, please reach out via our Contact page or email info@baselok.com.
