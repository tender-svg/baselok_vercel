Market Overview

Innovative Solutions for Advanced Roadway Construction

Baselok revolutionizes roadway construction with geosynthetic solutions that reinforce and stabilize subgrades for paved streets and roadways.  We mitigate reflective cracking, prevent rutting, and significantly extend the life cycle of highways, municipal streets, and even unpaved roads while reducing material costs.  Backed by University & third-party testing, and in ground plate load testing, our engineers design with confidence.

Project Highlights
  • Asphalt Reinforcement
  • Subgrade Stabilization
  • Unpaved Road Construction
  • Steep Slopes & Retaining Walls

Crack Mitigation

Reflective cracking destroys asphalt pavements prematurely. Baselok provides specialized asphalt reinforcement grids that dissipate stress within the pavement layer.

This significantly delays crack propagation, extending the life of asphalt overlays and reducing long-term maintenance costs.

Subgrade Stabilization

Building roads over weak soils leads to rapid deterioration. Our geogrids interlock with the base aggregate, creating a stiffened, load-distributing mattress.

This stabilization allows for a reduction in the required aggregate thickness, saving money and accelerating construction.

Unpaved Road Construction

Unpaved rural and industrial roads are prone to severe rutting and washouts. Baselok stabilizes these roads, providing a durable, all-weather driving surface.

Our solutions drastically reduce the need for continuous grading and aggregate replacement.

Steep Slopes & Retaining Walls

Highway construction often requires cutting into hillsides or building elevated embankments. We reinforce Mechanically Stabilized Earth (MSE) walls and steep slopes.

Our high-strength geogrids ensure the structural integrity of these critical earth retaining structures.
