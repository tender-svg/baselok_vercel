About Us

Who We Are

Baselok, by Industrial Fabrics, Inc., is a leading provider of geosynthetic solutions engineered for demanding civil and environmental applications. From roadway subgrade stabilization to coastal erosion protection, our products deliver proven performance across a wide range of industries including infrastructure, railways, marine, and environmental sectors.

With decades of engineering expertise and a commitment to innovation, Baselok partners with contractors, engineers, and project owners to solve complex ground stabilization challenges — reliably, cost-effectively, and sustainably.
