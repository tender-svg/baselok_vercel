Frequently Asked Questions

We offer a comprehensive range of geosynthetic solutions including geogrids, geocells, & geotextiles. Our product lines are engineered for applications across roadways, railways, marine environments, slopes, retaining walls, and foundation support systems.

Product selection depends on several factors including soil type, load requirements, design life, and application type. We recommend downloading our product selection guide or contacting our technical team for a project-specific recommendation. Our engineers are available to review your geotechnical data and assist with specification.

Our products are tested and certified to meet ASTM, ISO, and GRI standards. Product-specific test data, including tensile strength, aperture size, and junction efficiency, are available in each product's technical datasheet. Third-party lab reports can be provided on request for project specifications.

Standard products are typically available within 5-10 business days depending on your location and order volume. Custom specifications or large project quantities may require 3-6 weeks. Contact our sales team early in your project timeline to confirm availability and arrange logistics.

Our geocell systems are engineered from HDPE and are resistant to UV, saltwater, and biological degradation, making them highly effective for marine scour protection, shoreline stabilization, and underwater slope reinforcement. We have active installations in coastal, tidal, and deepwater environments worldwide.

For complex projects, our engineering team can provide full design support including FEM analysis and project-specific design reports.
