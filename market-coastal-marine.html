Innovative Solutions for the Coastal / Marine Waterways Industry

BaseLok Marine technologies showcase a line of dependable composite marine structures, which are leading the way in coastal restoration and living shoreline construction. We incorporate top-quality geogrids and geotextiles to create products designed to enhance the constructability and longevity of coastal and marine structures. 

Our innovative products and years of expertise in marine construction make us the easy choice for your next coastal restoration project. All marine mattresses, geogrid gabion baskets and geocomposite marine products, are manufactured in house and are American made.

Shoreline Protection

Coastal erosion threatens valuable land and infrastructure. Baselok systems, including specialized geocells and marine mattresses, provide resilient armoring against wave action. Our Marine Geocomposites provide scour protection and reinforcement for hard armor revetment and stone breakwater structures.

These solutions absorb and dissipate wave energy, significantly reducing sediment loss and shoreline retreat.

Underwater Foundations

Weak silt-laden marine spoils require specialized stabilization attention. Baselok is the only geosynthetics manufacturer with the capabilities of designing with our own in-house manufactured Foundation Performance System (FPS). Our customized geogrid composites system combined with Baselok geocell create a stiffened platform over silty and saturated sub-grade conditions, oftentimes encountered in coastal port facilities.

This allows for the secure placement of breakwaters, groins, and pier foundations without excessive settlement.

Port & Harbor Pavements

Coastal port facilities take a pounding from heavy fork lift and container reach stackers. These massive loads from shipping containers and heavy machinery require well thought out engineering and designs.  Our engineering professionals utilize quality Baselok geogrids, Fabgrid, and geocell, to overcome these extreme wheel loads.

The utilization of Baselok prevents rutting in container yards and ensures that the loads and traffic frequency requirements of your facility are met.

Dredged Material Containment

Managing dredged materials effectively is a major challenge in marine projects. Baselok provides high-strength geosynthetic reinforcement to protect breakwater structures and geosynthetic dewatering tubes.

These systems allow for efficient dewatering and safe containment of sediments, often repurposing them for land reclamation.
