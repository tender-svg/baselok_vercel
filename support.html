Temporary working platforms and crane pads are critical components of major industrial site development, renewable energy installations, and heavy civil construction projects. For modern project delivery teams, balancing structural bearing capacity over low-bearing soils with tight capital budgets is essential.The BaseLok® Engineering Team features highly experienced Professional Engineers (PEs) specializing in high-load mechanical stabilization. SLOPE & EROSION

Slope Stabilization & Erosion Control

Mitigating severe erosion control concerns along steep slopes, drainage channels, and shorelines requires careful evaluation of complex hydraulic variables. Geotechnical designs must account for critical factors such as peak flow velocity, boundary shear stresses, and hydraulic tractive forces.The BaseLok® GeoCell cellular confinement system is a premier solution for these demanding applications, offering unparalleled design flexibility through adaptable infill material configurations. Whether your project requires a hard-armored concrete invert for high-velocity channels, vegetated side slopes for natural stormwater management, or aggregate-filled steepened embankments, our dedicated team of Professional Engineers are here to guide you.
